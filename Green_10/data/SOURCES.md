# Sample news and photo credits

All nine stories, bylines, and news agencies in `dummy-data.js` are fictional classroom data. Dates are sample publication dates. Photographs illustrate the story topics; they do not document the fictional events or imply that their photographers endorsed the stories.

Images load from HTTPS links on Wikimedia servers. No news photos are bundled in `assets`. The app resizes and crops them for display; the source files have not been edited.
